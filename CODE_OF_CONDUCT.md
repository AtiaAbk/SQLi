# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

## Our Standards
Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:
- Being welcoming to newcomers, especially students and beginners
- Giving constructive, specific feedback on contributions
- Focusing on what is best for learners using this repository
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experience levels

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Sharing or requesting content that facilitates unauthorized access to systems
- Any conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional/educational setting

## Scope-Specific Standard
Because this is a security-education repository, we hold an additional standard: **no content, discussion, or request that facilitates unauthorized access to systems the requester doesn't own or have explicit permission to test.** This applies to issues, PRs, and discussions alike.

## Enforcement
Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainers via GitHub Issues (for non-sensitive matters) or direct contact listed in the repository owner's profile. Maintainers will review and respond to all reports and may remove, edit, or reject contributions that don't align with this Code of Conduct.
